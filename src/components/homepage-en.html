Before your first question

Any questions?

All FAQs (Russian) ↗
Is this another chat or a separate AI?

No. Ligos connects your service data to a compatible AI client. You ask questions in the interface you already use.

What can I learn by connecting different sources?

Compare stock and sales with a delivery discussion in Telegram, or compare planned receipts with marketplace payouts. What you can learn depends on the available data.

Can I receive reports automatically?

You can request a report using a saved prompt. Scheduled reports require a scheduling feature in your AI client or a separate automation.

Can the AI make mistakes?

Yes. It may suggest a hypothesis or misinterpret information. Check the source figures, reporting periods and conclusions before making important decisions.

Why I built Ligos

My company grew.
My visibility did not.

As my company grew, so did the number of people, systems and work chats. To understand what was happening, I had to question employees, open dashboards and read through conversations. It took time, and I was still relying on other people’s accounts.

Reports do not always reveal a mistake, a misunderstanding between colleagues or a problem nobody has resolved. I wanted to spot these things without spending half the day in dozens of chats.

I wanted to wake up, pick up my phone and ask: “What needs my attention today?” A walk through the whole company, in one question.

So I built Ligos: I connected different sources to my AI so it could compare information and help me see where I needed to step in.

Message me with any questions. I will be happy to help you connect your services and work through your use case.
